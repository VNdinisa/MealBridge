# Contact and closure

## Scripts (dispatcher reviews before sending)
For an older adult: greet by name, say the meal is still coming, who is bringing it (new volunteer first name), the new time window, what to do (e.g. "just answer the door, no action needed"), and a callback number. Short sentences, no jargon, calm tone. Provide a phone script and a short text version, plus a "no answer" voicemail line.
For a volunteer: stop list, addresses, windows, special notes, a request to reply "accept".

## Contact attempts
- Log each attempt: time, method, outcome (`reached`, `no_answer`, `wrong_number`, `declined`).
- Retry rules: second attempt after 15 min, third by a different method, then escalate to the dispatcher as owner with "needs welfare check" flag per program policy. Never silently stop.

## Closure note (factual only)
`Stop S07 | delivered 1:42pm by V3 | recipient reached by phone 1:50pm | no follow-up` or the follow-up needed. Do not invent details. Closing requires delivery outcome and contact outcome.

## Older-adult review checklist (Omotayo)
Plain words, no urgency pressure, no scam-like phrasing (never ask for money or personal details), large-print friendly, offers a human callback.
